## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-octane stadium energy combined with VIP broadcast streaming sophistication. Drawing inspiration from modern live OTT football platforms, it combines pitch-black darkness with blinding, electric canary gold accents (#FFD500) and atmospheric golden stadium floodlight glows. 

The aesthetic is technical, dynamic, and unapologetically bold—built for rapid live-score readability, low latency tension, and cinematic video immersion. Native bilingual orchestration seamlessly unifies Latin typography with RTL Arabic script balance (supporting IBM Plex Sans Arabic/Tajawal structures), maintaining identical hierarchy, razor-sharp edge contrast, and visual equilibrium in both reading directions.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around high contrast to ensure immediate glanceability under varying ambient conditions:

- **Primary (`#FFD500`):** Electric Voltage Yellow. Used exclusively for focal touchpoints—live badges, primary action triggers, active tabs, scores, and highlight indicators.
- **Secondary (`#111111`):** Deep Pitch Charcoal. The foundational container color for match cards, rail items, modal trays, and navigation surfaces.
- **Tertiary (`#D4AF37`):** Stadium Gold. A desaturated metallic tone reserved for secondary badges, championship accents, and diffuse stadium radial bloom effects.
- **Neutral Background (`#000000`):** True OLED Pitch Black. Eliminates light bleed and frames 4K HDR live match video.
- **Typography & Tonal Neutrals:**
  - Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`): Headlines, scores, active tab labels, and primary metadata.
  - Silver Grey (`#A3A3A3`): Match commentary, secondary labels, upcoming schedule timestamps.
  - Muted Graphite (`#242424`): Structural divider strokes, unselected track lines, and skeleton states.

## Typography

IBM Plex Sans anchors the typography system, pairing directly with its companion script IBM Plex Sans Arabic (or Tajawal fallback) without visual disruption. 

- **Display & Headlines:** Set in bold weights (`700`), punchy and condensed in presence, emulating stadium scoreboard typography.
- **Numbers & Scores:** Always render using tabular figures (`tnum`) to eliminate jitter during real-time game clock updates and score changes.
- **RTL Script Behavior:** When rendering Arabic text, `letterSpacing` is reset to `normal` across all styles to avoid breaking connected letter glyphs. The vertical metrics and line heights automatically compensate for Arabic ascenders and descenders.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an edge-to-edge, content-first fluid grid structured for multi-platform consumption across mobile, web, and TV screens:

- **Desktop & Smart TV:** 12-column grid system with `2rem` outer edge margins and `1rem` column gutters. Horizontal carousels snap fluidly to standard 16:9 thumbnail ratios.
- **Mobile (Handheld):** 4-column layout utilizing `1rem` margins and `0.75rem` gutters. Edge-peek carousels leave 12% of the next card exposed to encourage horizontal gesture discovery.
- **RTL Fluidity:** Layout matrices, side panels, and horizontal scroll tracks mirror automatically along the horizontal axis. Left-to-right timeline progressions reverse seamlessly into right-to-left match clocks without layout breaks.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses tonal surface step-ups and targeted atmospheric luminous lighting rather than traditional drop shadows:

- **Base Layer:** Pure Black (`#000000`).
- **Surface Layer 1 (Cards, Drawers):** `#111111` with a hairline stroke border (`1px solid #222222`).
- **Surface Layer 2 (Floating Player, Modals, Overlays):** `#1A1A1A` paired with a delicate gold ambient glow (`0 8px 32px rgba(255, 213, 0, 0.12)`).
- **Stadium Floodlight Effect:** Featured hero items and live banner cards incorporate an atmospheric radial gradient overlay: `radial-gradient(ellipse at top center, rgba(255, 213, 0, 0.15) 0%, rgba(0, 0, 0, 0) 70%)`.
- **Live State Glow:** When a match is live, active badges and borders carry an electric pulse shadow: `0 0 12px rgba(255, 213, 0, 0.45)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains an athletic, structured silhouette with a soft `roundedness` rating (Level 1).

- **Standard Containers & Cards:** `0.25rem` (4px) to `0.5rem` (8px). Keeps cards sharp, compact, and aligned with standard broadcast on-screen graphics (OSGs).
- **Match Badges & Chips:** Pill styling (`rounded-full`) is strictly reserved for LIVE indicators, match halves, and compact filter chips to contrast against the sharp rectangular frame of video player thumbnails.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (e.g., "Watch Live", "Subscribe"):** High-visibility `#FFD500` background with pure black (`#000000`) bold typography. Hover elevates luminosity with a subtle golden glow aura.
- **Secondary Action (e.g., "Highlights", "Remind Me"):** `#111111` fill with a `1px solid #333333` border and crisp white text. Yellow border accent on focus or hover.
- **Icon Buttons:** Pitch black circle with subtle grey border, icon tinted white, turning `#FFD500` on active or toggle states.

### Cards (Match & Stream)
- **Structure:** 16:9 or 2:3 ratio frames built on `#111111` backing. 
- **Overlays:** Dark bottom gradient (`linear-gradient(180deg, transparent 40%, rgba(0,0,0,0.9) 100%)`) ensures team names, score, and minute markers stay legible over live footage.
- **Live Indicator Badge:** Placed in top-left (top-right in RTL). High-vis `#FFD500` fill with black text and an animated pulsing red/yellow dot.

### Match Chips & Filter Tags
- **Default:** Transparent background with `1px solid #2B2B2B` border, text set in `#A3A3A3`.
- **Selected:** Solid `#FFD500` fill with black text or black fill with a prominent `#FFD500` outline and white typography.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Surface:** `#141414` background with a subtle inset stroke (`#242424`).
- **Focus:** Crisp border transition to `#FFD500` accompanied by a `0 0 8px rgba(255, 213, 0, 0.25)` halo. Placeholder text in muted silver `#666666`.

### Scoreboards & Timers
- High-contrast module with stadium-grade tabular display numbers. Live match minute highlighted in `#FFD500`; standard aggregate times in crisp white.
